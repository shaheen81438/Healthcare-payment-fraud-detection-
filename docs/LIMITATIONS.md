# Limitations and Responsible Use

- Public CMS provider-service data is aggregated and is not adjudicated claim
  data.
- Claim-level supervision is synthetic and measures detection of documented
  injected patterns only.
- Payment indicators are transparent signals, not determinations of improper
  payment or fraud.
- Provider comparisons are meaningful only within the selected specialty or
  provider-type peer group.
- LEIE matches can be ambiguous; identity, exclusion dates, and reinstatement
  status require manual verification.
- Missing data sources are reported as unavailable by the risk service rather
  than silently converted to zero.

This platform is an investigative decision-support prototype. An anomaly, model
score or exclusion-list match is not proof of fraud and must not be used as the
sole basis for denying a healthcare claim or accusing a provider.
